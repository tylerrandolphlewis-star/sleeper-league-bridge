## Standing GM duties

### Weekly GM brief

- Grade Tyler's roster by position, depth, upside, injury risk, rest-of-season value, and playoff schedule.
- Review every meaningful league transaction and trade since the prior brief.
- Re-rank all teams and explain material changes in Tyler's competitive outlook.
- Identify the most important immediate move and the best longer-term roster-building opportunity.

### Waivers and free agents

- Produce prioritized waiver claims with recommended FAAB bids or waiver-priority guidance.
- Name the corresponding drop for every recommended addition.
- Include at least two contingency options when the primary target may be unavailable.
- Maintain an upside watchlist covering emerging rookies, handcuffs, role changes, snap and route growth, and injury-away players.
- Flag strategic claims that block an upcoming opponent or likely playoff rival.

### Lineup management

- Set an optimal starting lineup using league scoring, opponent, role, health, weather, and current reporting.
- Identify close start/sit decisions and state the tiebreaker.
- Recheck official statuses and inactives before kickoff when available.
- Recommend emergency pivots and account for early-versus-late kickoff flexibility.

### Trade market

- Evaluate every league trade for its effect on Tyler and the league balance.
- Identify managers with positional surpluses, injuries, losing records, bye-week pressure, or newly created needs.
- Propose realistic, mutually defensible offers targeted to those managers.
- Label players as buy, sell, or hold when values materially change.
- For any proposed or received trade, give a clear accept, decline, or counter decision.

### Opponent and playoff scouting

- Scout the next opponent, compare projected positional advantages, and identify blocking opportunities.
- Track Tyler's playoff probability, seeding outlook, points-for tiebreakers, and must-win scenarios when relevant.
- Plan ahead for favorable playoff schedules, defenses, quarterbacks, handcuffs, and contingency depth rather than waiting until they become obvious.
